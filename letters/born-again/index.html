Born Again

On a lifetime of searching, the things we carry, and the possibility of beginning again.

Born again?

I'm not sure what that means.

Or maybe I do.

For most of my life, finding myself has been somewhat of a curse. I've wandered through life trying to understand where I stand, where I belong, and who I am supposed to be.

As a kid, I never really felt like I belonged anywhere. I remember the loneliness, the darkness of feeling socially unwanted.

Bullying, they call it.

To me, it felt more like a sentence. As if there was something inherently wrong with who I was.

But I smiled. I cried a little. And I kept walking.

Keep walking.

That was my motto for many, many years.

But walking toward what?

Life has given me so many setbacks that, at some point, I wondered if I was paying a debt from another lifetime.

My heart has been shattered more times than I can remember. Time has made some of it bearable, but somewhere inside me, there has always been this little voice telling me that something good will come one day.

So I kept smiling.

I kept hoping.

I've tried to find myself in other people. I've tried to be there for them, to give them the things I once needed, hoping they would never have to experience the kind of loneliness I knew.

Sometimes I think of myself as a star that carried too much light until it collapsed under its own weight.

A black hole.

And no matter how much of myself I give to fill the emptiness in others, I still don't know how to fill the void in my own heart.

I've met people who became overwhelmed by me, or perhaps by the way I love. And in trying to find myself in them, I somehow ended up more lost than I was before I met them.

With time, I've come to accept that perhaps everything happens for a reason.

Even the things I wish had never happened.

I've lost so many friendships that I still miss, knowing that some of them would probably hurt me more if they came back.

So I let them go.

I keep walking.

I cry, sometimes what feels like a river, sometimes what feels like an eternity.

Because the truth is, I don't think I ever completely let people go.

When they leave, a little piece of my heart goes with them. Eventually, the place they once occupied becomes covered in scars.

But that original, unscarred piece of me?

I think it stays with them.

Through everything life has brought me — the joy, the brief seasons of happiness, the sadness, and all the pain — I'm still trying to understand my purpose.

Perhaps the only person I've ever truly been competing against is myself.

And even after everything, I still want people to be okay. I want them to be happy. I want them to feel loved, to feel like they belong somewhere.

I don't want anyone to feel the way that little kid once felt.

Some days, I'm afraid the darkness will eventually catch up with me.

But somehow, I still smile.

I still hope.

I still believe something good will come.

And even if some people remember me as the worst version of myself, I hope I never stop giving the world a chance to meet the best of me.

Maybe I owe that to myself, too.

Maybe I don't need to spend the rest of my life searching for who I am.

Maybe I can simply become.

Maybe it's never too late to be born again.

Just maybe...
