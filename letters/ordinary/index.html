A solitary star · November 11, 2026

Ordinary

On being someone to someone, and wondering if you were ever the same to them.

I think the saddest part
is realizing I might have been
just another person in your life.

Because you were never
just another person in mine.

And maybe that's why this hurts so much.

Because I believed you
when you said you cared.
When you told me I mattered.

And I held those words close,
perhaps closer than I should have.

Not because I needed constant reassurance,
but because they came from you.
And words have a way of becoming precious
when they come from someone
who means something to your heart.

I never expected to mean
everything to you.

But I thought I meant something.

Something that would show
in the little things.
In the effort.
In the moments when words weren't enough.

I thought there was a certain kind of care
that didn't need reminding.
The kind that remembers you
even when you're not standing right in front of it.
The kind that makes room for you,
not out of obligation,
but simply because you're someone
they want in their life.

Because that's how I cared about you.

You crossed my mind in ordinary moments.
In things I wanted to tell you.
In little stories I saved without even realizing it,
because I thought you'd want to hear them.

And maybe that's what makes this so difficult.

It wasn't some grand expectation
I had placed on our friendship.

It was the small, quiet belief
that somewhere in your days,
I existed the way you existed in mine.

Not constantly.
Not above everyone else.
Just enough to be remembered.
Enough to be considered.
Enough for your words to feel true.

And I think I spent too long
trying to understand the distance
between what you said
and what you showed me.

Making excuses for the silence.
Finding meaning in the smallest gestures.
Holding on to moments of warmth
because I didn't want to believe
they were all I was going to get.

Maybe you did care.
Maybe you still do.

I don't want to take that away from you.

But there is something deeply lonely
about being told you're important
and still having to wonder
where you stand in someone's life.

And I keep wondering
if I misunderstood our friendship,
or if something changed along the way.

I don't know which one hurts more.

I think a part of me
is still trying to make peace
with the possibility
that something I held so dearly
was something you held so lightly.

Not because it meant nothing.

But because it never meant
quite the same thing.

I just know it's a strange kind of sadness,
feeling like you're losing someone
who doesn't seem to notice
you're drifting away.
