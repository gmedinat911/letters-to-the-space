Going Rogue

On wanting to become cold, and being afraid of losing the warmth that remains.

A heart trying to grow cold, while something inside it refuses to stop burning.

I think being too open made me easier to hurt.

I gave pieces of myself to people without thinking twice. I trusted them with parts of me I probably should have protected.

And maybe that was my mistake.

Maybe my intensity was always going to be too much for some people. Maybe the way I cared, the way I showed up, the way I wanted to feel close to others was something they never really knew what to do with.

But I never thought being open could feel so lonely.

It's strange.

I've spent so much of my life wishing I could be colder. Wishing I could stop caring so deeply. Wishing I could become someone who doesn't feel every silence, every distance, every little change in the way someone treats me.

But my heart has always been too warm.

No matter how much ice I try to build around it, somehow, it melts.

And despite everything, my first instinct is still to be kind.

I hate that sometimes.

Because there are days when I genuinely don't want to care anymore.

But somewhere inside me, there's still this foolish little hope that the world could be a place where nobody has to feel alone.

And maybe that's what hurts the most.

Sometimes I wonder if people laugh at how much of myself I give.

Look at him.

Always caring too much.

Always believing people mean what they say.

Always hoping that if he gives enough of himself, someone will finally make him feel like he belongs.

How naive.

And maybe they would be right.

Because sometimes it feels like the world wasn't made for people who wear their hearts so openly.

I don't know if the people who hurt me will ever understand what they did.

Maybe they'll never think about it again.

Maybe they'll move on with their lives, while I'm still trying to understand how something that meant so much to me could have meant so little to them.

Maybe one day I'll see them and feel nothing.

I hope so.

Because right now, it hurts to remember how freely I gave pieces of myself, only to feel like those pieces were discarded when they were no longer convenient.

And the saddest part is that those pieces of me never deserved that.

I know they didn't.

So maybe this is the lesson.

Stop showing everyone your cards.

Stop loving people more than they love you.

Stop giving so much to people who only reach for you when they need something.

Give what they give.

Or maybe less.

Less feels safer.

Less doesn't leave so much of you behind when someone walks away.

And I hate that I'm even writing this.

Because I never wanted to become someone who measures kindness. Someone who keeps score. Someone who has to think twice before showing another person that they matter.

But I'm tired.

Tired of being the one who feels everything long after everyone else has moved on.

Maybe this time, something in me will finally change.

Maybe the fire in my heart will grow a little dimmer.

Maybe the ice I've spent so long trying to build will finally be strong enough to hold.

Maybe today was the last lesson I needed before I learned how to close myself off completely.

Or maybe tomorrow I'll wake up and still be the same person.

Still caring.

Still hoping.

Still foolish enough to believe that somewhere, someone will know what to do with a heart like mine.

I don't know which possibility scares me more.

Maybe we'll find out.

Until then...
