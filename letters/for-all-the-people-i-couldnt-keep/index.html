For All the People I Couldn't Keep

Some constellations are only meant to hold for a little while.

I've been thinking about how strange it is to miss someone who is still somewhere in this world.

Someone who hasn't really gone anywhere, except perhaps away from the place they once held in yours.

And I don't really know what to call that.

Maybe it's goodbye. Maybe it's acceptance. Or maybe it's simply learning that not everyone we love is meant to walk beside us through every season of life.

Lately, I've been thinking about the people who made my life a little brighter just by being in it.

The conversations, the laughter, the stupid little moments that probably meant nothing to anyone watching, but somehow meant the world to me.

And I am grateful.

God, I am so grateful.

But I would be lying if I said that gratitude makes the sadness disappear.

Sometimes I wish people could see themselves through my eyes. Just for a moment. To understand how precious they were to me.

I've always cared deeply. When someone becomes important to me, I want them to know it.

I never wanted anyone to feel obligated to have me in their life. I just wanted to feel that they wanted me there.

And maybe that's why it hurts when a friendship that once felt so natural becomes something you start to question.

When you have a thought, a story, or a little piece of your day that reminds you of someone, and then realize you don't quite know if you should share it anymore.

I'm learning that people have their own ways of caring. Their own limits, their own lives.

That someone can genuinely care about me and still be unable to offer the friendship I hoped for.

I can respect the space someone needs and still feel sad about it.

I can miss someone without asking them to come back.

So, to the people who have been part of my life, who made ordinary days extraordinary without even realizing it:

Thank you.

For the laughter, the memories, and the little pieces of yourselves that you shared with me.

For letting me know you, even if only for a season.

And maybe the beautiful thing about having known someone isn't how long they stayed, but that, for a little while, our lives found each other.

I'm glad we laughed.

I'm glad we shared the time we did.

Maybe that's what acceptance looks like.

Being grateful that, for a little while, it happened at all.

Thank you for being here, even if only for a little while.
