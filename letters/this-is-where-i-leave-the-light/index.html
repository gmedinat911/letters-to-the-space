This Is Where I Leave the Light

I think I understand the light now.

For a long time, I thought it was pointing somewhere.

Toward a person.

Toward another life.

Toward some answer I hadn't reached yet.

I kept looking into the distance because something was still shining there, and I assumed that if the light remained, there must still be somewhere to go.

Maybe that was the mistake.

Not the feeling.

Not the love.

Not even the hope.

Just believing that every light is meant to guide us somewhere.

Some light only shows us where we've been.

And some, I think, exists so we can finally see ourselves.

I have spent so much of my life looking outward.

At who stayed.

Who left.

Who remembered.

Who chose me.

Who reached back when I reached first.

I measured my place in people's lives by the space I seemed to occupy when I wasn't standing in front of them.

Maybe because somewhere inside me there has always been this quiet question:

Would anyone notice if I disappeared?

And I think I have spent years trying to answer it through other people.

By being useful.

By remembering everything.

By showing up.

By loving loudly.

By becoming someone difficult to forget.

Maybe that's why absence has always felt so enormous to me.

Maybe that's why silence can become a language in my head.

Maybe that's why I sometimes hold onto people long after I should simply let them exist beside me.

Because somewhere underneath all of it, I wasn't only afraid of losing them.

I was afraid of what losing them might say about me.

That maybe I wasn't important enough.

Memorable enough.

Loved enough.

Enough.

And there it is.

The thing underneath so many other things.

I wanted someone else to prove that I mattered.

But maybe that was never their job.

Maybe the light I've been chasing wasn't leading toward another person at all.

Maybe it was illuminating the parts of myself I had stopped knowing how to see.

The softness.

The ridiculous sentimentality.

The person who remembers a sentence someone said six months ago because somehow it mattered.

The person who cries when something is beautiful.

Who gets excited about stupid little things.

Who makes things with his hands because buying something doesn't always feel like enough.

Who loves people deeply and sometimes has absolutely no idea where to put all of that love.

I spent a long time thinking those parts of me were liabilities.

Too emotional.

Too attached.

Too much.

So I tried to become easier.

More collected.

More independent.

Someone who needed less.

But I don't think becoming whole means needing nothing.

I think it means no longer being ashamed of the person doing the needing.

There is a version of me underneath all of this who simply wants to be seen.

And maybe I kept asking other people to look at him because I had stopped looking at him myself.

I see him now.

Not perfectly.

But enough.

I see the person who wants freedom and closeness at the same time.

Who can walk alone and still hope someone chooses to walk beside him.

Who wants to be remembered because he remembers everyone.

Who sometimes gives too much because giving is easier than admitting he wants something back.

Who has confused being chosen with being worthy.

Who has confused being missed with being loved.

Who has confused intensity with destiny.

And I don't hate him for any of it.

He was trying to find his way home.

Maybe that's what all of this has been.

Not a love story.

Not a tragedy.

Not even really an ending.

A lighthouse.

Something bright enough to make me look toward the horizon until, eventually, I realized the shore beneath my feet was mine.

I don't need to extinguish the light.

That's the part I finally understand.

I don't need to pretend certain people didn't matter.

I don't need to turn tenderness into embarrassment because it didn't become what I imagined.

I don't need to rewrite love as a mistake just because it couldn't tell me where to go.

The light can remain.

Gold can remain gold.

Memories can remain memories.

Love can remain love.

None of those things require me to follow them.

I can simply carry what they illuminated.

And keep walking.

There will still be days when I look backward.

There will still be people I miss.

There will still be silence that makes some old part of me wonder whether I mattered.

When that happens, I hope I remember this.

I was there too.

Through every version of it.

Through every person who came and went.

Through every future I imagined.

Through every goodbye I thought would destroy me.

Through every time I changed myself hoping someone would stay.

I stayed.

Maybe I should start giving that some weight.

Maybe being remembered by everyone else was never the only proof that I existed.

I remember me.

I know what I carried.

I know how deeply I loved.

I know how hard I tried.

I know the pieces of myself I lost.

And now I know some of the pieces I am finding again.

So maybe this is where I leave the light.

Not with anyone else.

Not at the end of some road.

Not inside a possibility that never became real.

Here.

With me.

Because maybe afterlight was never supposed to show me how to find my way back to someone.

Maybe it was waiting for me to realize that I was the one I was trying to find.
