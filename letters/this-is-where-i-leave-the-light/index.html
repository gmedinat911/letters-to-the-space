This Is Where I Leave the Light

I think this is the last letter I will write to you.

Not because there is nothing left to say. Maybe there will always be something left. A forgotten sentence. A memory that returns while I’m driving. A song I still instinctively want to send you. Some ordinary moment that makes me think, for just a second, that I should tell you about it.

But I think I have finally understood what afterlight is.

It is the light that remains after its source has already disappeared. It looks close enough to touch, even though it is coming from somewhere that no longer exists in the same way.

And maybe that is what I have been living inside.

The warmth left behind by something beautiful, mistaking its glow for a place I could still reach.

What we had was real to me.

The drives. Running beside you. Coffee. Stupid conversations. The little things you said that stayed with me longer than you probably realized. All the ordinary moments that somehow made me feel remembered and a little less invisible.

I didn’t invent those moments.

And I don’t need to erase them now simply because they didn’t become what part of me imagined they could become.

But somewhere inside that closeness, I created a possibility.

I wondered whether there was another life waiting quietly beneath this one. A life in which circumstances changed, the distance between us disappeared, and you stepped toward me.

I let that possibility become brighter than it had any right to be.

Bright enough that sometimes I stopped seeing what was actually in front of me.

You never promised me that other life.

When everything became real, you stepped away. You asked me to step back too, to return to the life in front of me and come back when I was ready.

It hurt.

Because some part of me had hoped that would be the moment you reached for me.

But you didn’t do anything wrong.

You showed me where the edge of our story was, even when I wasn’t ready to see it.

And now I have to respect that edge.

Because somewhere along the way, I let the possibility of another life pull me away from the one I was actually living. I let myself keep looking toward a door that had never really been opened.

I don’t want to live that way anymore.

There are people, places, and pieces of myself that deserve my presence here.

Not halfway here while some imagined future remains quietly lit somewhere behind me.

So after one final promise I intend to keep, I’m going to let there be distance between us.

Not to punish you.

Not to make you miss me.

Not as one final attempt to see whether my absence makes you reach for me.

I cannot turn my goodbye into another question for you to answer.

This is only me accepting the answer that life has already given me.

You were gold to me.

You still are.

I hope one day, when something reminds you of me—a run, a race, a stupid joke, a song, maybe even a bag of coffee—you remember that there was someone who saw something extraordinary in you.

Someone who believed in you.

Someone who cared about you.

Someone who was genuinely grateful that your path crossed his.

Maybe I don’t need to know whether you remember me anymore.

I hope you do.

But I don’t need to stay close enough to find out.

You don’t have to come looking for me.

You don’t have to explain anything.

Just smile, if you can.

And remember me.

This is where I leave the light.
