The Goodbye You’ll Never Hear

Maybe this is where I have to say goodbye.

Not to you, not really. I don’t think I could look at you and say goodbye, and I don’t think I want you to know that somewhere inside of me I am doing it. There doesn’t need to be a final conversation, or a last message, or some beautiful collection of words that explains everything I have felt.

Maybe this goodbye is only mine.

Something I say quietly, from my heart, while wishing you well from somewhere you cannot hear me.

I hope life is kind to you. I hope you keep running toward the things you want. I hope you laugh a lot. I hope you find people who make you feel understood and safe and loved. I hope you get everything you have told me you wanted and even some of the things you never told me about.

I hope you’re happy.

And I think I have to learn how to hope for all of that without needing to be there to see it.

Because standing here hurts.

Standing with my heart open, hopeful, waiting for you to reach toward me hurts. Looking into all this silence and wondering whether somewhere on the other side you’re thinking about me hurts. Trying to understand whether I matter to you by looking at messages, conversations, little moments, all of it hurts.

And I’m tired of searching for an answer that may never come.

Will you remember me?

I hope so.

Will you see something one day and, without meaning to, think of me?

I don’t know.

Will something make you laugh and remind you of something stupid I once said? Will you drink coffee and remember me bringing you a bag from somewhere far away? Will you stand before a race one day and remember how much I believed in you? Will something happen and, for one second, will you instinctively want to tell me?

I don’t know.

Will you miss me?

I don’t know that either.

Sometimes I doubt you will, and that thought breaks something very quiet inside of me because I know that I will remember you.

I already do.

I remember the little things. I remember things you probably don’t know I kept. I remember because I listened to you, because I wanted to know you, because somewhere along the way knowing you became important to me.

Maybe that’s why I wanted so badly to be remembered by you.

When I told you that I hadn’t seen my mom in three years, there was a part of me that wondered whether that sentence would stay with you. Whether sometime later you would remember that my mom is everything to me. Whether you would wonder how seeing her felt. Whether, for just a moment, something important to me would become important to you simply because it belonged to me.

Maybe you will remember.

Maybe you already have.

I can’t know what happens inside your mind when I’m not there, and maybe that’s something I have to finally accept.

Because I have spent too much time standing here wondering whether you will reach for me.

And the truth is that my heart is tired.

I don’t want to keep holding it open and waiting. I don’t want to keep hoping every silence will eventually become your voice. I don’t want to keep searching for myself inside the things you do or don’t do.

I think I just have to let go.

Not because I stopped caring about you.

That’s what makes this so hard.

I’m letting go while I still care.

I’m walking away from the waiting while there is still a part of me that wants to turn around and see if you’re coming.

Maybe that’s what goodbye really is sometimes. Not leaving because love disappeared, but leaving because you can no longer stand in the same place waiting for love to return in the way you need it.

So I won’t ask you to remember me.

I won’t ask you to miss me.

I won’t ask you to come looking for me.

I’ll just hope.

Quietly.

I’ll hope that somewhere inside you I left something behind. Maybe not something enormous. Maybe not enough to change anything. Just a small piece of me that survived after I stopped being part of your everyday life.

And if one day, years from now, something ordinary brings me back to you for a second, I hope you smile.

You don’t have to be sad.

You don’t have to wonder where I am.

You don’t have to wish things had been different.

Just remember that there was someone who cared about you very deeply. Someone who wanted good things for you even when he didn’t get to be part of them. Someone who remembered you.

And maybe in another life we meet each other differently.

Maybe in another life there isn’t so much silence.

Maybe in another life I don’t have to wonder whether you remember me.

But in this one, I did know you.

I did care.

I did remember.

And for whatever time I had a place beside you, I was grateful to be there.

Now I think I have to leave that place without knowing whether you’ll notice it’s empty.

So this is my goodbye.

You will probably never hear it.

I’ll carry it quietly.

And wherever you go from here, I hope life is beautiful to you.

Remember me, if you can.

I’ll remember you.
