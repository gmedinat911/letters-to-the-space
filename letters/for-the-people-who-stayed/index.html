A Red Giant · November 19, 2026

For the People Who Stayed

For everyone who kept making room for me, even when I forgot to look around.

I think I've spent a lot of time thinking about the people I've lost.

The friendships that changed, the conversations that stopped, and the people I thought would be part of my life for much longer.

And somewhere in all that sadness, I think I forgot to look around.

I let the absence of one person become louder than the presence of so many others.

I spent so much time wondering why someone stopped choosing me that I almost forgot to appreciate the people who kept choosing to be part of my life.

And that makes me a little sad.

Because while I was looking at everything I'd lost, you were right there.

Making me laugh. Inviting me places. Asking how I was doing. Sharing little pieces of your lives with me.

Just being my friends.

And I wish I'd looked around sooner.

Because there are people here who have made my life so much better without even realizing it.

Some of you came into my life somewhere between early mornings, miles that weren't particularly impressive, and conversations that somehow made the distance feel shorter.

Others through ridiculous jokes, conversations that made absolutely no sense, and a shared talent for pretending to be nonchalant about things we probably care about more than we admit.

Some of you were supposed to be just people I worked with.

Names on a screen, meetings on a calendar, conversations that were supposed to be about work.

But somewhere along the way, we started talking about life instead.

And somehow, people I once called coworkers became people I was genuinely grateful to call friends.

And some of you simply showed up one day, and without me noticing when it happened, became people I couldn't imagine my days without.

I don't think I've told you enough how much that means to me.

You've seen different versions of me.

The loud one. The ridiculous one. The one who gets overly excited about the smallest things and sometimes cares a little too much.

But you've also seen the quieter one.

The one who overthinks, who sometimes struggles, who doesn't always know how to ask for help.

And in your own ways, you've made room for him, too.

Sometimes it was a message. An invitation I didn't know I needed. A stupid joke at exactly the right time. Someone checking in just because they wanted to.

Sometimes it was someone listening when things weren't making much sense. Someone telling me the truth, even when it wasn't what I wanted to hear.

And sometimes it was just a little grace when I couldn't find much of it for myself.

I wonder if you know how much those moments meant to me.

Because I remember them.

And I hope you know that I don't love you just because you've been there for me.

I love who you are.

Your humor. Your kindness. Your weird little habits. The things you're passionate about. The way you laugh, the stories you tell, and the different ways you make the world a little less lonely.

I love getting to celebrate your victories, listen to your problems, make fun of you when you deserve it, and be someone you can count on, too.

Because I don't just want friends who are there for me.

I want to be a good friend to you.

And I hope I have been.

I know life changes.

People get busy. Sometimes the miles become fewer, the conversations less frequent, and the things that brought us together start to look different.

I don't know what all our tomorrows will look like.

But I know that right now, I'm grateful you're part of mine.

And maybe that's something I've been learning lately.

I don't want to become so afraid of losing people that I forget to love the ones I still have.

I don't want to spend so much time grieving someone's absence that I overlook the people who are making my days worth remembering.

And I certainly don't want to wait until someone becomes a memory to tell them how much they meant to me.

So this is for you.

For the ordinary moments that turned into memories. The laughter, the miles, the long days, the terrible jokes, the conversations about absolutely nothing.

For the friendships that started somewhere unexpected and became something I never knew I needed.

For all the little things you probably don't remember doing, but that I haven't forgotten.

Thank you for letting me be part of your lives.

I hope I've made yours a little better, too.

And I hope there are still so many stories we haven't told, places we haven't gone, and memories we haven't made.

I love you guys.

I'm really glad we found each other.

And I'm even more grateful that we're still here.
