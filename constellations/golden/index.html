Constellation · August 10, 2026

Golden.

Dearest Friend,

Sometimes my mind does these weird mindfucks.

Maybe because I’m afraid of losing gold. Or maybe diamonds, like you.

But actually, no.

I think you’re gold.

I’m afraid of losing people I’ve given a big place in my heart. And sometimes that fear gets so loud that I lose sight of what is actually important.

And what is important is you.

How precious you are to me. How much your presence has meant in my life. How somehow, just being you, you uplift my spirit.

And I want to be careful with that sentence. Because uplifting me is not your responsibility. Making me happy is not your responsibility. Staying because I’m afraid of losing you is definitely not your responsibility.

Your presence just does that on its own.

I’m not really sure when you became this important to me.

Maybe it wasn’t one moment. Maybe it was runs, stupid conversations, laughs, serious conversations, random days, and a hundred little things I wasn’t keeping track of.

But somewhere along the way, you became someone incredibly precious to me.

And I’m not going to lie. The fear of losing someone you care about can freeze you. Part of me is living that right now.

And when you’re already afraid of losing one person, the thought of losing another feels exponentially worse.

So when distance appeared, everything felt different. Silence felt different. Space felt cold. And things that maybe weren’t meant to hurt me, hurt me.

I became so afraid of losing you that I started looking at you through that fear instead of looking at you.

And that wasn’t fair.

I’m sorry.

Because underneath all that fear, there’s something I know with absolutely no confusion:

You are golden to me.

I was going to call you a diamond because diamonds are rare.

But no.

Gold feels more like you.

Maybe gold isn’t the rarest thing in the world, but somehow everything has always revolved around it. It has value simply because of what it is.

And that’s you to me.

Golden.

I hope my actions haven’t pulled you away. I really do.

But I’m also learning that loving someone means accepting that they get to choose how close they want to stand.

And if my actions did pull you away, or if one day you simply decide that your place in my life needs to be farther from mine, I know it will hurt. Probably more than I want to admit.

It’ll leave a little hollow place in my heart where something really beautiful used to live.

But you will still be gold to me.

Because your value to me was never supposed to depend on whether you stayed.

So yes, I hope you stay.

I hope there are more runs. More stupid conversations. More laughs. More ordinary days I don’t realize are important until later.

But more than anything, I hope I never let the fear of losing you make me forget how grateful I am that I got to know you in the first place.

I love you, dear friend.

Whether you’re here, whether you’re a little farther away, or whether one day you decide to leave.

You’ll still be golden to me.
