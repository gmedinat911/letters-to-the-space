LETTER I OF VI

Maybe Tomorrow

I almost reached out today.

Just saw something that reminded me of you.

I opened our messages.
Then I scrolled up
to see who had texted last.

I hate that I did that.

When did saying hello
become something I had to think about?

There was a time when I could send you anything.
A joke. A bad day.
Some nonsense from my afternoon.

I never wondered
if I was bothering you.

I just knew I could.

And that's the strange part.
Nothing really happened.

No argument.
No goodbye.

You're still kind.
We still talk.
Sometimes it even feels the same.

Until it doesn't.

Maybe life is just busy.
Maybe I'm noticing a distance
you don't even know is there.

And maybe that's what scares me.

Not that you're leaving.
But that I might be the only one
feeling you go.

I miss how easy it was.
How I never needed a reason.
How I never had to check.

Anyway,
I closed our messages.

I don't even remember
what I wanted to tell you anymore.

I just remember
that I didn't.

Maybe tomorrow.
