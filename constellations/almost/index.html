LETTER I OF VI

Maybe Tomorrow

I almost reached out today.

Saw something that reminded me of you.

Nothing important.
Just one of those little things
I used to send without thinking.

For a second, I could almost hear your laugh.

I miss that.

I miss the nights we'd talk about a show
like we personally knew everyone in it.

The opinions. The jokes.
How something so unimportant
could turn into a whole conversation.

I miss how easy it felt.

And I know I was usually the one
who started those conversations.

But you were there too.

You laughed.
You shared your thoughts.
You remembered things about me
I didn't even know you were paying attention to.

I don't want to pretend
those moments weren't real
just because I wish there had been more of them.

I remember when you reached out, too.

The birthday message.
You telling me you missed me.
The little things that made me feel
like I crossed your mind sometimes.

I held on to those moments.

Maybe because they made the silence
a little easier to understand.

I think that's where things got confusing.

When you reached out,
I felt chosen.

Even when it was for a run,
a workout,
or something you needed help with.

I was happy you thought of me.

And I genuinely loved those moments.

But somewhere along the way,
I started wondering if you ever missed me
when there wasn't anything we needed to do.

I didn't mind being needed.

I just wanted to feel wanted, too.

And when life got difficult,
I wanted to turn to you.

Not because I expected you to fix anything.

I just wanted my friend.

Someone to listen.
Maybe a hug.
A little message asking how I was doing.

But I knew you needed space.

And I wanted to respect that.

So I stopped asking.

Not because I didn't need anyone.
But because I didn't want to feel
like I was becoming a bother.

I think that's when something changed for me.

I started thinking twice
before reaching out to someone
I used to talk to without a second thought.

And still, I waited.

Hoping maybe you'd reach out first.

Not to prove anything.
I just wanted to know
if you ever felt the distance too.

Eventually, I reached out anyway.

I told you I missed talking to you.

And when you answered,
I think we were having
two different conversations.

You were explaining why we'd been busy.

I was trying to tell you
that I missed my friend.

Maybe you didn't realize
how much I was trying to say.

Maybe I didn't know how to say it.

But I remember reading your response
and feeling my stomach drop.

I was hurt.

And I was angry at myself
for having hoped things would be different.

Because part of me had felt this distance
long before I finally said anything.

I just kept hoping I was wrong.

And maybe that's the hardest part.

I still remember the good things.

Your laugh.
Your voice.
The conversations about nothing.
How happy I was when you reached out.

I don't want to lose those memories
just because things didn't turn out
the way I hoped they would.

But I also don't want to forget
how lonely I sometimes felt
trying to keep us close.

Anyway.

Something reminded me of you today.

And for a moment,
I forgot all of that.

I just wanted to tell you.

I opened our messages.

Then I scrolled up
to see who had texted last.

I wish I didn't think about things like that.

I wish saying hello
still felt as simple as it used to.

But I closed our messages.

And went on with my day.

Maybe tomorrow.
